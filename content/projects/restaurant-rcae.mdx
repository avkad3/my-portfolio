## Overview

Full-stack restaurant reservation platform with an embedded AI research component — the Reservation Conflict Anticipation Engine (RCAE).

## Features

- **Conversational Booking**: Natural language reservation via AI assistant
- **RCAE Engine**: Deterministic conflict prediction using 4 operational stress metrics
- **Capacity Management**: Real-time table optimization

## Research Publication

Published on Zenodo (CERN) — DOI available. The RCAE introduces novel explainable operational stress prediction with open-source implementation.

## Impact

- Reduced overbooking incidents significantly
- Research-validated approach to scheduling conflicts
