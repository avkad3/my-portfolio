## Overview

Production-grade demand forecasting platform delivering probabilistic predictions with interpretability features for enterprise supply chain decisions.

## Models

- **Temporal Fusion Transformer**: Deep learning with multi-horizon attention
- **LightGBM**: Gradient boosting baseline with fast inference
- **Conformal Prediction**: Rigorous confidence intervals without distributional assumptions

## Impact

- 34,000+ product-plant time series processed
- Multi-horizon forecasts: 1, 6, and 12 months
- Enabled data-driven inventory decisions reducing stockout rates
