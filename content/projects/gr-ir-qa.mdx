## Overview

Enterprise-grade question-answering system for Goods Receipt / Invoice Receipt reconciliation documents. Enables automated compliance querying and document intelligence.

## Architecture

- **ChromaDB Vector Search**: Semantic retrieval over chunked documents
- **Azure OpenAI**: GPT-4 for answer generation with source attribution
- **PDF Pipeline**: Extraction, chunking, and embedding generation

## Results

- 94% semantic accuracy on reconciliation Q&A
- Reduced manual document review time by 60%
