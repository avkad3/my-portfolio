## Overview

Developed a custom reinforcement learning environment for autonomous satellite orbital transfers. The PPO agent learns fuel-efficient low-thrust maneuvers, outperforming classical Hohmann transfer solutions.

## Results

- **100% success rate** after 500K training steps
- **12% better fuel efficiency** vs. classical Hohmann transfers
- Generalizes to varying initial orbits and target altitudes

## Technical Details

- Custom Gymnasium environment with continuous action space
- Physics simulation using orbital mechanics equations
- Reward shaping for fuel efficiency + mission success
